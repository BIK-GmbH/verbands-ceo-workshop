| Task cluster | Typical tasks |
|---|---|
| Strategy & annual planning | Annual goals, topic planning, association development |
| Board & committees | Preparing meetings, resolution drafts, follow-up |

| PR · newsletter · web · social | Press work, circulars, website, social media |
| Knowledge management, bylaws, resolutions | Resolution status, bylaw questions, rulebooks, Q-mark documents, filing |
| Administration · data protection · compliance | Office, contracts, insurance, data protection, permissions |
