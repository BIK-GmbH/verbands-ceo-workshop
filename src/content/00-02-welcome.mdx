<En>
We start with **our questions**, not with a solution. Nobody is bringing a finished concept today that we would only have to nod through.

These two days are a **<Hint de="Diskussionsorientiert und durchgehend im Plenum. Einzige Ausnahme sind die kurzen Einzelinterviews zu Beginn von Phase 1." en="Discussion-based and entirely in plenary. The only exception are the short one-to-one interviews at the start of phase 1.">working session</Hint>**. We bring in our experience from the association and the industry, develop the relevant questions from it and look for the answers together. The facilitation holds the frame, the content comes from everyone.

**At the end there will be:**

1. a **shared, reasoned answer** to the key question “AI managing director: fiction or reality?”,
2. a **<Hint de="Sieben Poster, von Need to Move bis Commitment. Sie hängen sichtbar im Raum, werden parallel digital erfasst und lassen sich später als Vorlage oder mit Inhalt drucken." en="Seven posters, from need to move to commitment. They hang visibly in the room, are captured digitally in parallel and can later be printed blank or with content.">poster per phase</Hint>** that makes our result visible,
3. a **<Hint de="Alle Eingaben aus den Folien, Notizen und Interview-Ergebnisse laufen live zusammen. Das Protokoll lässt sich als PDF, Word, Markdown oder JSON exportieren." en="All input from the slides, notes and interview results come together live. The record can be exported as PDF, Word, Markdown or JSON.">record</Hint>** with all contributions as documentation of the results.

> The answer may be “reality”, “fiction” or something in between. What matters is that it is **ours**.
</En>
