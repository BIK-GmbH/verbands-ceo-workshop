**Three guiding questions:**

1. Who am I, and what is my role in the association or in my company?
2. What do I spontaneously associate with the title “<Hint de="Ein bewusst zugespitzter Arbeitstitel. Ob und in welcher Form eine KI Aufgaben der Geschäftsführung übernimmt, unterstützt oder gar nicht berührt, erarbeiten wir erst." en="A deliberately pointed working title. Whether and how AI takes over, supports or does not touch management tasks is what we work out.">AI managing director</Hint>”?

We pick up the expectations again in phase 7 and check whether they have been met.">Thursday lunchtime</Hint> for the two days to have been worthwhile for me?
