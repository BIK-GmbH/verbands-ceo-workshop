    en="Directly funding an innovative association-CEO operating model is harder than funding digital transformation or qualification. We therefore recommend a two-step logic: first use qualification and digitalisation programmes, in parallel set up a pilot with a research or transfer partner."

Ohne Gemeinnützigkeit bzw. bei stärkerem Demonstrations- und Transferfokus ist die bessere Logik ein Pilot mit Hochschule, Forschungspartner oder EDIH über Horizon Europe / Digital Europe. Bayern-spezifisch ist die direkte Förderlage für einen e. V. schwächer."
    en="Funding takeaway: with non-profit status, 100xDigital and — prospectively — transform_D are the most obvious direct candidates. Without non-profit status, or with a stronger demonstration and transfer focus, the better logic is a pilot with a university, research partner or EDIH via Horizon Europe / Digital Europe. Bavaria-specifically, the direct funding situation for an e. V. is weaker."

</NoteCard>
